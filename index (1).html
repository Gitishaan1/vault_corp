Hints (click to reveal, one at a time)
  1. Open DevTools before you log in. Watch the Network tab as you submit the OTP form — not every request needs a real server.
  2. After OTP verification, check document.cookie in the Console. Does that value look plain, or encoded?
  3. Try decoding it (atob(...)). Once you know the format, can you set a different cookie value yourself?
  4. Getting into the portal as "admin" by cookie isn't the whole challenge — the vault checks a signed token too. View the page source (Ctrl+U) for anything odd near the top.
  5. That odd comment is base64. Decode it — it tells you what kind of secret to look for and its value.
  6. With the secret and algorithm (HS256) known, forge a JWT with {"role":"admin"} using any online tool like jwt.io, and store it: localStorage.setItem('token', YOUR_JWT). Then click "Open Vault" again.
